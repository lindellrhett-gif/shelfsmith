Privacy Policy

Last updated November 22, 2026

The short version.

  • We collect what the app needs to work: your account, your household's pantry and shopping list, and your preferences.
  • We never sell your information, show you ads, or track you across other apps and websites.
  • Receipt photos are sent to Anthropic's AI to read the items, only after you allow it, and we delete each photo as soon as it has been read.
  • Diets and allergies are stored only with your separate permission, and are erased if you withdraw it.
  • You can delete your account, and your data with it, from inside the app at any time.

Who we are

Shelfsmith is operated by Bluestem Software LLC ("we", "us"), who is responsible for your personal information as its controller. Contact us about anything in this policy at support@bluestemsoftware.com.

Receipt scanning

Receipt scanning is optional and asks for your permission before it is first used.

  • When you scan a receipt, the photo is uploaded to our servers and sent to Anthropic, the company that makes the Claude AI, which reads the items and returns them to us.
  • Receipts can show more than groceries: the store and its location, the time of purchase, items that say something about your health, such as medicines, and sometimes part of a payment card number or a loyalty programme name. Please bear this in mind when choosing to scan.
  • Anthropic processes the photo on our behalf under its commercial terms, which do not permit it to use the photo to train its AI models.
  • We delete the photo from our servers as soon as it has been read, whether or not reading succeeded. Sometimes a photo is uploaded but not read, for example if the app is closed during the upload or the scan is refused. We then delete it the next time a receipt is read for your household once the photo is an hour old, or when the last member of your household deletes their account. If the last member leaves the household instead, the photo is not deleted automatically: email us and we will delete it.
  • We keep the items read from a receipt, with the store name and date, for up to 90 days so that you can finish reviewing it. Like the pantry, it is visible to your household. Nothing is added to your pantry until someone confirms it.
  • You can withdraw permission at any time in the app under Settings, then Privacy. Adding items by hand works without it.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information, share it for targeted advertising, or allow anyone to track you. We share it only as follows.

  • Your household. Members of a household you belong to see its pantry, shopping list, meal plan, cooking history, member names and roles.
  • Service providers who process information on our behalf, under contracts that limit them to providing their service to us and require them to protect it:
    • Supabase: database, sign-in, file storage and server functions.
    • Resend: sending account emails, such as confirmation and password reset codes. Resend receives your email address and the content of the email.
    • Anthropic: reading receipt photos, only when you use receipt scanning.
    • RevenueCat: managing subscriptions and restoring purchases. When you sign in on an iPhone, the app gives RevenueCat your account ID, whether or not you subscribe, so that any purchase is linked to your account.
    • Apple: delivery of notifications, if you turn on reminders.
    • Expo: routing notifications to Apple, if you turn on reminders.
  • Apple and Google sign-in. If you choose Sign in with Apple or Sign in with Google, that company confirms who you are to us, under its own privacy policy. We do not send them your pantry, lists, preferences or anything else you keep in Shelfsmith. If you delete an account you created with Sign in with Apple, we also ask Apple to disconnect Shelfsmith from your Apple ID.
  • Apple. If you buy Shelfsmith Plus, you buy it from Apple through the App Store. Apple takes payment and handles the purchase under its own privacy policy.
  • Legal reasons. If required by law, or where necessary to protect the rights, safety or property of our users, the public or us.
  • Business transfer. If the app is sold or transferred, your information may pass to the new operator, who must continue to protect it as this policy describes. We will tell you before that happens.

How long we keep it

  • Receipt photos: deleted as soon as they have been read, as described above.
  • Items read from a receipt, with the store name and date: up to 90 days.
  • Diets and allergies: until you withdraw permission or delete your account.
  • Your account, preferences and favourites: until you delete your account.
  • Your privacy choices: the time you allowed receipt reading, or the storing of diets and allergies, is kept until you withdraw that permission or delete your account. Whether reminders are on is kept with your preferences until you delete your account.
  • Subscription status: until you delete your account. When you delete your account, we also ask RevenueCat to delete your customer record. Apple keeps its own purchase records under its own privacy policy.
  • Notification tokens and time zone: a token is kept until you delete your account, or until a reminder cannot be delivered because Shelfsmith is no longer on that device. Your time zone is kept with your preferences until you delete your account. Turning reminders off stops the reminders but does not delete the token or the time zone.
  • Shared household data: pantry items, list entries, meal plan entries and cooking history stay with the household while it has members, because the other members rely on them. If you delete your account, anything you added stays with the household but is no longer linked to you. If you leave a household, anything you added stays with it and is still marked as added by your account. A household is deleted, with its pantry, lists, meal plan, cooking history and the items read from its receipts, when its last member leaves.
  • Backups and logs: our hosting provider keeps backups and server logs for a limited period, after which deleted information is overwritten. This is normally no longer than 30 days.

Your choices and rights

Wherever you live, you can:

  • See and correct most of your information directly in the app.
  • Delete your account in the app under Settings, then Privacy, then Delete account. This permanently deletes your account and personal information, as described above.
  • Withdraw permission for receipt scanning or for storing diets and allergies in Settings, at any time. You can also turn off reminders in Settings while your household has Shelfsmith Plus. Without Plus, reminders are not sent, and you can turn off Shelfsmith's notifications in your iPhone's Settings at any time. Withdrawing does not affect anything done before.
  • Ask for a copy of your information, or ask us any question about it, by emailing us. We send copies by email.

Depending on where you live, you may have further rights, including to object to or restrict how we use your information, to have it transferred to you in a portable format, and to complain to a regulator. We respond within the time the law allows, normally 30 days, and may need to confirm your identity first. We will not treat you differently for exercising your rights.

Automated decisions

We do not make decisions about you by automated means that have legal or similarly significant effects on you. The app does sort and filter recipes automatically, using your pantry and preferences, and an AI reads your receipts. You are free to ignore any suggestion, and nothing read from a receipt is added to your pantry until someone in your household confirms it.

Security

Information is encrypted in transit and at rest, access is restricted so that each household can reach only its own data, and passwords are stored only as secure hashes. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security, but we will notify you and the relevant regulators of a breach where the law requires.

Children

Shelfsmith is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes, we will tell you in the app or by email before they take effect, and update the date at the top. If we want to use your information for a purpose this policy does not describe, we will tell you before we start. Where a change needs your consent, we will ask for it.
