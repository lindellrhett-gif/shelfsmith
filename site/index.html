Shelfsmith

Cook what you already have. Shelfsmith keeps track of your household's pantry, shows the recipes you can make right now, and builds your shopping list for the rest.

Get help

Email lindellrhett@gmail.com and we will reply as soon as we can, usually within two working days. Telling us your iPhone model and what you were doing when something went wrong helps us fix it faster.

Common questions

How do I share a pantry with my household?

Open Settings and share your household's invite code. When someone joins with it, you see the same pantry and shopping list, and changes appear on every phone straight away.

Why is a recipe missing from my list?

Recipes are hidden if they conflict with your diets or contain an ingredient you excluded, or take longer than your time limit. Check Settings. Recipes you are one or two ingredients short of appear under "Almost there".

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Shelfsmith. Deleting the app or your account does not cancel a subscription.

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles refunds for App Store purchases. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

What happens to my receipt photos?

With your permission, each photo is sent to Anthropic's AI to read the items, and we delete it as soon as it has been read. The full details are in the Privacy Policy.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, open Settings, then Privacy, then Delete account. This permanently deletes your account and personal information. If you cannot open the app, email us from the address on your account and we will delete it for you.
